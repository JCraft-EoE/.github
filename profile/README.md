# JCraft: Eyes of Ender
JCraft: Eyes of Ender is a mod for Minecraft Java Edition 1.20.1 that adds more than 25 stands, various specs, and many other items and features from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure to the game.  
On top of our main mod, JCraft: Eyes of Ender, we've also got [JJBA Cosplay](https://github.com/JCraft-EoE/jjbacosplay) which lets you cosplay as your favorite characters, and [JJBA Music](https://github.com/JCraft-EoE/jjbamusic) which adds noteblock covers of your favorite JoJo soundtracks.

Consider joining [the Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/mFRbQmqrmc) and downloading [the mod from CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jcraft-eyes-of-ender) or downloading [the mod from Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/jojo-eyes-of-ender).

If you like our work, consider [supporting us](https://ko-fi.com/jcrafteyesofender).
